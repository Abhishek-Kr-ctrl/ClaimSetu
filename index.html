                    Insurance Act 1938 — Section 45
                    Complete indisputability after 3 years from policy inception / revival.
                    Repudiations based on undisclosed pre-existing diabetes or hypertension after 36 continuous months are legally barred.
                  

                  

                    Insurance Ombudsman Rules 2017
                    Pecuniary jurisdiction for claims up to ₹50 Lakhs; awards binding on insurers.
                    Zero court fee, speedier than Consumer Courts (NCDRC), forces insurer compliance within 30 days of award.
                  

                  

                    IRDAI PPI Regulations 2024 / Master Circular
                    Insurers must acknowledge within 3 days and resolve grievance within 15 days.
                    Sets an explicit countdown on the GRO representation letter before filing with Ombudsman.
                  

                  

                    IRDAI Proportionate Deduction Guidelines
                    Associated medical expenses (ICU, implants, tests) cannot be slashed arbitrarily.
                    Generates evidence demands requiring itemized proof before accepting room-rent linked haircuts.
                  

                  

                    Cashless Everywhere 2024 Mandate
                    Cashless facility in any hospital with 15+ beds regardless of network.
                    Empowers appeals against arbitrary denials on "non-network hospital" pretexts.
